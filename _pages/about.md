Welcome! I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the **Massive Stars Group** at the University of Potsdam, Germany. Before moving to Potsdam, I spent some wonderful time with the **Hot Milk team** at INAF–Osservatorio Astronomico di Brera, Italy. My academic journey began at **Nanjing University**, China, where I earned both my B.S. and Ph.D.

My research focuses on high-energy astrophysics, particularly X-ray source populations, compact binaries, massive stars, and stellar feedback. I use observations from **XMM-Newton, Chandra, and eROSITA** to investigate the Galactic X-ray source populations, the origin of the Galactic Ridge X-ray Emission, and high-energy processes in low-metallicity star-forming galaxies. I am also interested in X-ray variability and time-domain astrophysics, including periodic sources and active galactic nuclei.
